New Venture Strategy · Team project

CarePlus 365: a continuous care strategy

CarePlus has patients. It doesn't yet have relationships.
Market sizingSubscription strategyGo-to-market
View the full deck
Problem

An urban diagnostic & primary care chain with no repeat engagement

CarePlus is episodic (patients visit only when sick), fragmented (no data continuity across visits) and transactional (no recurring revenue model). Across marketing, operations, finance and consumer insight, the root cause is the same: CarePlus is a clinic — not a care relationship. Trust-driven category, low digital presence between visits, no post-visit follow-up, and revenue that spikes with illness and collapses between cycles.

The recommendation

A two-tier membership model built on continuous care

Shift from episodic transactions to a relationship subscription — value-led, with no discounts or one-time offers. “Your clinic knows you.”

TierPriceWhat's included
BasicFreeOnline booking, first consultation free, digital health onboarding, Health Score access
Pro₹199/yrPriority booking, My Doctor continuity, reduced diagnostic rates, tele-consultation, full health dashboard

Patient journey: Enroll → Basic → Health Score → Nudge → Pro → Follow-up → Renew.

Product ecosystem

What CarePlus builds

App
Digital front door: booking, health records & onboarding
Health Score
Vital-tracking index that creates a habit loop for all users
My Doctor
Named preferred doctor — emotional anchor, Pro-only retention feature
Tele-Consultation
Pro-tier; bridges physical visits with convenience
Post-Visit Follow-Up
WhatsApp/app messages with results-in-context and next steps
Care Dashboard
Full health trend tracking, test history and appointment log
Highest-value segments

Chronic disease and targeted niches

Diabetes, hypertension and thyroid disorders are urban India's silent epidemic — these patients visit 3–4× per year by necessity, and CarePlus serves them via Health Score trend tracking, tele-consultations, My Doctor continuity and post-visit alerts. Women's Health is the lead niche campaign: cervical & breast cancer screening and PCOD management, a high-trust, underserved, high-loyalty segment — only ~2% of eligible women are diagnosed despite 40% of cancer among Indian women being breast or cervical (NCBI), and 20% of women suffer PCOD symptoms with 40% undiagnosed (ThinkGlobalHealth).

Financial logic

Bangalore market sizing: 14M → 3M SOM

Total population14M
Relevant market8.68M
SOM (serviceable)3M
Revenue before app deployment~₹72 Cr
Revenue post app deployment~₹131 Cr

Before: 3M SOM × ~20% = 600K users, 600K × ~80% visit rate × avg. test cost (₹1,500), driven by walk-in procedure fees alone. After: ~30% of 700K convert to paid Basic (100K increase via marketing), Basics at ~2.5 visits/yr × avg. test cost (₹1,300) plus subscription fee, casual remainder at ~70% visit × test cost — an ~82% revenue uplift vs. the base scenario (indicative estimate for an urban city like Bangalore; actual figures vary with pricing, penetration & mix).

Way forward
  • AI Health Assistant: 24/7 symptom query resolution in local languages, chronic condition nudges, automated abnormal-result alerts.
  • Predictive Health Nudges: flag patients likely to lapse on preventive visits before they need acute care.
  • Corporate Wellness Partnerships: bulk membership deals with large urban employers — a B2B channel that scales without marketing spend.
  • Women's Health Hub: dedicated wing with OB-GYN partnerships and screening camps.

Problem statement

Two core issues undermine the experience

01 · Recommendation engine gap
Homepage relies on category bifurcation only — no personalisation. Every user sees a near-identical landing page.
02 · Ads during paid entertainment
Paid subscribers still see ads while watching movies and series, undermining perceived value.
Solution 1

AI-powered personalised homepage

Signals: watch history & completion rate, favourite genres/languages/actors/sports, search history, time of day & device, trending among similar users. A Tamil movie fan sees Kollywood releases and “Similar to Karuppu”; a Marvel fan sees the Marvel Universe; a cricket fan sees match highlights and team-specific videos — faster discovery, higher engagement, better long-tail utilisation.

ERP consulting partner for a home décor manufacturer

EcoHome Décor designs, manufactures and sells handcrafted and contemporary home décor — wall art, lighting, vases, soft furnishings and furniture accents — through showroom, D2C, marketplace and B2B/hospitality channels, combining a central manufacturing unit with a network of artisan workshops across 8 departments. Business process overview

Three BPMN models capture how EcoHome Décor runs

Order-to-Cash (O2C)
Quotation → sales order → pick/dispatch or trigger manufacturing → invoice → payment reconciled, one real-time view across Sales, Warehouse and Finance.
Procure-to-Pay (P2P)
Material shortfall → RFQ → purchase order → goods receipt & inspection → 3-way match → payment released.
Custom / Bespoke Décor Order
Client brief → concept & costing → advance payment → production → QC → balance on delivery — a higher-margin workflow for interior designers & hospitality clients.

Karma Yoga Field Project · Noble Nayakas, Project 9 – Team 2

Rural entrepreneurship through native chicken rearing

Turning a backyard habit already practised in Kollamedu village into a guaranteed-buyer livelihood model.
Field researchSocial impactProject Lead
View the project folder
Where & who

Kollamedu village, Tirukkalukunram, Kanchipuram District

A compact, single-street community of roughly 45–50 households. Field visits ran across June–July 2026 (six visits), surveying households and mapping the existing informal, middleman-driven egg and poultry market. Local coordination ran through Ms. G. Devi, Sodexo Supervisor on the GLIM campus and primary point of contact in the village.

The business idea

Kollamedu Native Chicken & Egg Enterprise

Pooling native (country) chicken eggs — and later live birds — from about 10 participating households, and supplying them directly to the GLIM campus community and a partner store in town, bypassing middlemen through a POC-led, WhatsApp-coordinated collection system. It builds on an activity villagers already do (backyard poultry), needs very low capital per household, and uses resources they already have.

Problem it solves

A structured, guaranteed buyer at a fair price

For village households
A guaranteed buyer and fair price, instead of selling informally or to a middleman at a lower rate
For GLIM buyers
Fresher, farm-sourced native eggs than the supermarket alternative
Top cited problems
Predation by wild dogs/cats, and lack of a guaranteed market/fair price
Market & differentiation

No organised poultry business currently exists near Kollamedu

Households sell eggs informally and inconsistently (only 2–3 families do so regularly); one nearby farm (Kunnathur) only supplies chicks and isn't a competitor. This model is the first to pool eggs/birds from multiple households, guarantee an institutional buyer (GLIM), eliminate the middleman, and build toward community (Self-Help Group) ownership. Roughly 7–8 Kollamedu households have shown willingness to act as suppliers/co-producers.

Operating model

How it's produced, collected and delivered

Production
Households rear hens in their own backyards and collect eggs daily
Collection
POCs collect pooled eggs door-to-door every 3 days, logging quantities on WhatsApp
Delivery
Same-day delivery to a GLIM campus stall or a partner store in town
Resources needed
Native chicks (Kunnathur farm), predator-proof coops, basic healthcare kits, kitchen-scrap feed, egg trays
Project objectives

Rural livelihoods, built on what villagers already do

  • Promote sustainable rural entrepreneurship through native chicken rearing as a profitable livelihood activity
  • Improve income and employment for rural households, especially women and small farmers
  • Strengthen market linkages and value addition for native chicken products
  • Build a scalable, replicable model of rural entrepreneurship based on native poultry rearing
